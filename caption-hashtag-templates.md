---

## CAPTION RULES
1. First line = the hook (matches or extends the video hook)
2. Middle = 1–2 lines of value/story
3. End = one clear CTA
4. Emojis: 1–3 max, purposeful, not decorative spam
5. Length: 100–150 characters performs best on TikTok (captions are truncated after ~100 chars — put the CTA before the cut)

## MASTER HASHTAG REFERENCE

### Always use 1 of these (brand tags):
- `#iloKrafts`
- `#HandmadeMarketplace`

### High-volume craft/maker tags (use 1–2 per video):
- `#CraftTok` — 8B+ views
- `#HandmadeFinds` — 2B+ views
- `#MakerCommunity` — 500M+ views
- `#SmallBusinessTikTok` — 4B+ views
- `#HandmadeGifts` — 1B+ views

### Mid-volume niche tags (use 1 per video — matches the content):
- `#DIYCraft` — tutorials and process
- `#ArtisanMade` — premium feel
- `#ShopSmall` — shopping/commerce content
- `#EtsyAlternative` — high-intent buyers
- `#MeetTheMaker` — vendor spotlights
- `#HandmadeProcess` — behind-the-scenes
- `#CraftTutorial` — tutorials
- `#TikTokShop` — shop-linked content
- `#UnboxingTikTok` — unboxing videos
- `#WomenWhoCreate` — female vendor spotlights

### Trending tags (check weekly — rotate):
- Look at what's trending under `#CraftTok` and `#SmallBusiness` each Monday
- Add 1 trending tag to culture/trend content only
- Never use a trending tag that doesn't fit the video — TikTok penalizes misuse
