Pre-print checklist

Run through before sending to your DTF printer

  • ✓
    Background is transparent PNG — not white or solid colour
  • ✓
    Resolution is 300 DPI or higher
  • ✓
    File saved as PNG with alpha channel (transparency) — not JPG
  • ✓
    Colour mode is RGB for DTF printing
  • !
    Check design dimensions match your intended placement (chest, back, sleeve, pocket)
  • !
    Minimum 0.25" gap between all designs on gang sheet for cutting
  • !
    Confirm sheet size and orientation with your printer before uploading
  • ✓
    Designs with bold outlines and solid colours transfer best on fabric
  • ✓
    Save your AI prompt — you can regenerate variations anytime

Style notes

Halftone shading

Dot and line halftones mimic classic screen-print separation. AI renders these best with bold, high-contrast artwork.

Dark garment prints

For black or charcoal shirts, your artwork needs bright or light colours — avoid dark-on-dark designs.

Layer distress after

Generate clean halftone art first, then add distress texture in Photoshop or Procreate before DTF transfer.

Ink coverage

Heavy screen print = bold and opaque. Soft washed = lightweight feel, perfect for fashion-forward streetwear pieces.
