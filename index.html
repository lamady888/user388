Motherhood is beautiful.
It's also hard.

If you've ever struggled with postpartum depression, anxiety, or the silent battles of motherhood — you are not alone.

Erana Unfiltered is a six-episode documentary series that goes beyond the picture-perfect moments of new motherhood to explore the raw, honest truth that so many mothers experience but rarely say out loud.

We talk to mothers, partners, mental health professionals, and maternal care experts — because the more we speak, the less alone we all become.

About the Channel

Why This Series
Had to Be Made

Postpartum depression affects millions of mothers worldwide — yet the stigma, shame, and silence around it remains deafening.

Erana Unfiltered exists to change that. This series is for every mother who has smiled through the pain, felt invisible in her struggle, or wondered if she was broken. It's for partners who didn't know what to say. For families who didn't understand. For anyone who needs to hear that healing is real and possible.

We joined YouTube in March 2025 — and we're just getting started.

Support & Resources

You Don't Have to
Navigate This Alone

If you or someone you love is struggling with postpartum depression or maternal mental health, these organisations can help right now.

01

Postpartum Support International

24/7 helpline, local support groups, and an extensive resource library for mothers and families experiencing perinatal mood disorders.

Helpline: 1-800-944-4773
postpartum.net
02

National Maternal Mental Health Hotline

Free, confidential support before, during, and after pregnancy. Available 24/7 in English and Spanish from trained counselors.

Call or text: 1-833-943-5746
mchb.hrsa.gov
03

Crisis Text Line

Free, 24/7 text-based support for anyone in crisis. A trained crisis counselor responds — available whenever you need it most.

Text HOME to 741741
crisistextline.org
04

Talk to Your Doctor

Your OB, midwife, or GP is your first line of support. Postpartum depression is a medical condition — and it is treatable with the right help.

Schedule an appointment today
Ask at your next visit
This series is for awareness and community support. It is not a substitute for professional medical or mental health care. If you are in immediate crisis, please contact emergency services or a crisis line.
