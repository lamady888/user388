# Month 1 Content Calendar — @ilo.krafts
**Platform:** TikTok  
**Period:** Days 1–30 (Launch Month)  
**Goal:** 500 followers, establish content identity, test all 5 pillars  
**Posting time:** See schedule — always post within the recommended window

---

## HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT
Each video entry includes:
- **Pillar** — which of the 5 Cs it belongs to
- **Hook** — the first 2 seconds (memorize this, it's everything)
- **Script** — shot-by-shot breakdown with exact words to say
- **Caption** — copy-paste ready
- **Hashtags** — 3–5 targeted tags
- **CTA** — the one ask you make at the end

> Film 7 videos in a single batch session, then post across the week. Never scramble.
