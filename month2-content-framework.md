## WEEK 5 — "Grow the Community"

### Video 1 — Monday: Collab with a Craft Creator
**Goal:** Tap into their audience, introduce ilo.krafts to a new community.

**How to find your collab:**
- Search `#CraftTok` and filter by Most Recent
- Find a creator with 5K–50K followers (micro = higher engagement rate)
- Their aesthetic should match ilo.krafts — warm, handmade, real
- DM them: "Love your content — we'd love to feature one of your pieces on ilo.krafts and collab on a TikTok together. We handle the script, you just bring your craft."

**Collab video formula:**
```
[0–3s]   Their hook: they show a piece they made
[3–12s]  They describe the making process
[12–20s] You appear or your voiceover adds: "And you can find work like this on ilo.krafts"
[20–25s] Both accounts tagged, CTA to link in bio
```

---

### Video 2 — Tuesday: Audience Q&A Part 1
**Goal:** Turn Month 1 comments into content. Makes commenters feel heard, drives more comments.

**Format:**
```
[0–2s]   "You had SO many questions last month. Here are the best ones."
[2–8s]   Question 1 on screen + your answer (15–20 words max per answer)
[8–14s]  Question 2 + answer
[14–20s] Question 3 + answer
[20–25s] "More answers coming this week. Drop your question below 👇"
```

**Source your questions:** Go back to your top 5 most-commented videos and screenshot the most common/interesting questions.

---

### Video 3 — Wednesday: Vendor Recruitment Drive
**Goal:** Actively grow the supply side of the marketplace.

**Hook options:**
- "Are you a maker? You're leaving money on the table."
- "I need to talk to every crafter watching this."
- "We have buyers. You have crafts. Let's fix that."

**Script structure:**
```
[0–2s]   Hook (above options)
[2–10s]  What ilo.krafts offers vendors: "Real buyers, zero commission on your first [X] orders,
          a community that supports handmade."
[10–20s] Show a vendor success story — "[Name] made her first $500 in her first week."
[20–28s] CTA: "Comment 'VENDOR' below or tap the link in bio to apply.
          We're accepting [X] new vendors this month."
```

---

### Video 4 — Thursday: UGC Campaign Launch
**Goal:** Get buyers to create content FOR you — free reach, social proof.

**Launch video script:**
```
[0–2s]   "We're doing something we've never done before."
[2–10s]  "If you've ever bought from ilo.krafts — we want your video.
          Show us your purchase, your unboxing, your reaction.
          Anything."
[10–18s] "The best video this month wins [prize — free products, vendor credit, feature].
          Tag us @ilo.krafts and use #MyiloKrafts."
[18–25s] Show an example (use Day 29's customer UGC from Month 1 as the template).
          "It can look like this. Or anything you want."
[25–30s] "Winner announced [date]. Let's see what you've got."
```

**Prize options (low-cost):**
- Free products from your vendor catalog ($30–$50 in value)
- Their video featured as a pinned TikTok for 1 week
- A "Best Customer" vendor credit on your marketplace

---

### Video 5 — Friday: "What's New This Week" Product Drop
**Goal:** Create a weekly recurring format — predictable content that followers return for.

**Format (keep consistent every Friday in Month 2+):**
```
[0–2s]   "It's Friday. You know what that means."
[2–25s]  3 new or restocked products — same format each week:
          Product name | Who made it | Price | One thing that makes it special
[25–30s] "All at the link in bio. Same time next week."
```

**Why this works:** Recurring formats build appointment viewing. Followers start coming BACK on Fridays to see new drops. This is how you build a loyal audience, not just a reached one.

---

### Video 6 — Saturday: Craft Technique Deep Dive
**Goal:** The highest-save content format — education that people bookmark.

**Pick ONE specific technique to teach this week:**
- A joinery technique in woodworking
- A specific knot in macramé
- A particular stitch in embroidery
- A glaze technique in ceramics

**Format:**
```
[0–2s]   "This technique is what separates amateur work from professional work."
[2–40s]  Teach the technique in 4–5 specific, clear steps.
          Be specific: name the technique, explain WHY it matters.
          "Most people skip this — here's what it changes."
[40–50s] Show before/after: without the technique vs. with it.
[50–60s] "If you want this done professionally, this is what our vendors
          spend years practicing. Shop the link in bio."
```

---

### Video 7 — Sunday: Community Check-in
**Goal:** Weekly ritual that builds intimacy with the audience.

**Format:**
```
[0–3s]   "Sunday check-in. How's everyone doing?"
[3–15s]  Share 1 genuine thing that happened this week — a vendor milestone,
          a funny order story, something you learned, a challenge you faced.
          Be real. Vulnerability on TikTok drives connection.
[15–25s] Ask one community question:
          "What's the most meaningful gift you've ever received?"
          "What craft do you wish you'd learned earlier?"
          "What would you want to see more of from us?"
[25–30s] "Drop your answer below. I read every single one."
```

## WEEK 6 — "Behind the Brand"

### Theme rationale:
By week 5, followers know what you sell. Week 6 is about why you exist.
The most loyal TikTok communities form around brands that have a clear mission and a human behind them.

### Video lineup:
1. **Monday:** "Why I built ilo.krafts" — origin story, 45–60 seconds, raw and honest
2. **Tuesday:** "A day in the life running a handmade marketplace" — vlog style
3. **Wednesday:** "The hardest part of building this" — challenge/vulnerability post
4. **Thursday:** Trending audio + text that communicates your brand mission
5. **Friday:** Friday product drop (recurring, same format as week 5)
6. **Saturday:** Tutorial (your most-saved tutorial type from Month 1, repeated with new product)
7. **Sunday:** Community check-in + tease week 7

## WEEK 7 — "Customer Stories"

### Theme rationale:
Social proof at scale. This week every video centers on a real buyer's experience.
These videos get shared more than any other type because people tag the person they bought for.

### Video lineup:
1. **Monday:** Customer story — feature someone who bought a gift and the reaction they got
2. **Tuesday:** "I read your comments" — address the most common buyer questions
3. **Wednesday:** 3 most popular products this month (based on Shop data) — why people love them
4. **Thursday:** Trending audio — "Things ilo.krafts buyers say" (relatable identity content)
5. **Friday:** Friday product drop + tease next week's LIVE
6. **Saturday:** Tutorial using a product from your catalog (buyer-to-maker bridge)
7. **Sunday:** UGC contest reminder + winner announcement teaser

## MONTH 2 PLATFORM UNLOCKS TO PURSUE

### TikTok Creator Marketplace (if eligible)
- Access at: creator.tiktok.com
- Lets brands pay creators to make content about you
- Eligibility: 10,000+ followers
- Target: apply immediately when you hit 10K

### TikTok LIVE Gifts
- Viewers can send virtual gifts during LIVEs = real revenue
- Eligibility: 1,000 followers, 18+, account in good standing
- Turn this on as soon as you're eligible

### TikTok Shop Affiliate Program (launch in Month 2)
- Creators apply to sell your products for a commission (you set the rate)
- Recommended rate: 10–15% to attract quality affiliates
- Target: recruit 5 affiliates in their first 30 days
- These are creators who make content FOR you, for free, earning commission only when they drive sales

### TikTok Green Screen Effects
- Use the green screen effect to react to vendor content, products, or craft videos
- This duet-style format gets significantly more algorithmic push than standard videos

## IF YOU'RE AHEAD OF SCHEDULE

If you hit 2,000 followers before Day 60, do this immediately:
1. Apply for TikTok Creator Fund (if available in your region)
2. Post a "how we grew this fast" video — meta-content about your growth story performs extremely well
3. Reach out to your top 3 most engaged followers personally — these are your future super-fans
4. Start a TikTok Series (a grouped set of videos with a unifying theme) — TikTok promotes Series content differently
