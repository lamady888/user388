## 3. Content Pillars (The 5 Cs)

### Pillar 1 — CRAFT (40% of content)
Show the making process. Nothing stops a scroll like hands working on something beautiful.
- Time-lapse of a vendor making a product start to finish
- "You won't believe how this is made" hooks
- Material sourcing stories
- Before/after transformation videos

### Pillar 2 — COMMUNITY (25% of content)
Make followers feel they belong to something.
- Vendor spotlights: 30-sec "Meet the Maker" interviews
- Client unboxing reactions
- Comments-turned-content ("You asked for this tutorial...")
- Duets/stitches with craft creators

### Pillar 3 — COMMERCE (20% of content)
Drive traffic and sales without being salesy.
- "What I bought from ilo.krafts" POV videos
- Price reveal + value justification ("Handmade vs. mass-produced")
- Limited drops and countdown urgency
- TikTok Shop product demos

### Pillar 4 — CULTURE (10% of content)
Tap into trends to ride algorithmic waves.
- Trending audio + craft twist
- "POV: You're a craft vendor who just got their first order"
- Participate in relevant TikTok challenges (#CraftTok, #HandmadeFinds)

### Pillar 5 — CREDIBILITY (5% of content)
Build trust with proof and authority.
- Testimonials from buyers and vendors
- Media mentions / features
- Milestone celebrations (1K vendors, 10K orders, etc.)

## 5. Video Production Framework

### Hook Formula (First 2 seconds — make or break)
Use one of these openers every single video:
1. **Question hook:** "Did you know handmade earrings can cost less than $10?"
2. **Shock/contrast hook:** "This took 8 hours to make and sold in 3 minutes."
3. **Visual hook:** Start mid-process, no intro, no logo reveal
4. **POV hook:** "POV: You're shopping for a gift that actually means something"

### Video Structure (15–30 sec optimal)
```
[0–2s]   Hook (visual or audio pattern interrupt)
[2–10s]  The story / value / process
[10–20s] Payoff / product reveal / result
[20–25s] Call to action ("Link in bio", "Follow for more", "Comment your fave")
```

### Production Standards
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical (full screen)
- **Resolution:** 1080×1920 minimum
- **Captions:** Always — 40% of TikTok is watched without sound
- **Text overlays:** Large, readable font, contrasting color
- **Lighting:** Natural light or ring light — no dark videos
- **Audio:** Trending sounds OR clear voiceover (not both competing)

## 7. Engagement & Growth Tactics

### Algorithm Levers to Pull
1. **Watch time** — If viewers rewatch or watch >80%, TikTok pushes to more FYPs. Make content that rewards a second watch.
2. **Comments** — Ask one specific question at the end of every video to drive comments (comments = engagement signal).
3. **Shares** — Create "send this to someone who..." moments. Shareable content = exponential reach.
4. **Saves** — Lists, tips, and tutorials get saved. Saves are TikTok's highest-value signal.

### Daily Engagement Tasks (15 min/day)
- Reply to every comment within the first 2 hours of posting (TikTok rewards this)
- Leave 5–10 meaningful comments on accounts in the #CraftTok space (builds visibility)
- Stitch or duet 1 craft creator per week (collaborative reach)
- Pin your best comment on new videos to steer conversation

### Creator Collaborations
| Partner Type | Strategy | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Craft micro-influencers (5K–100K) | Send products for organic review | Authentic reach |
| TikTok Shop affiliates | Add products to affiliate program | Commission-driven traffic |
| Local maker communities | Co-create vendor spotlight content | Supply-side growth |

## 8. TikTok Shop Setup

TikTok Shop is the highest-converting commerce feature on the platform — activate it immediately.

### Steps
1. Apply at `shop.tiktok.com` → Marketplace seller account
2. List top 10–20 vendor products as catalog items
3. Tag products in every product-feature video
4. Enable **Affiliate Program** — let creators earn commission for driving sales (zero upfront cost)
5. Run **LIVE shopping sessions** once/week: showcase vendor products in real-time, answer questions, limited-time discounts

### Live Shopping Best Practices
- Go live Thursday or Friday evening (6–9 PM) for highest viewership
- Minimum 30-minute sessions (TikTok boosts longer lives)
- Co-host with a vendor for authentic storytelling
- Offer a live-only discount code ("LIVE10" for 10% off)

## 12. Crisis & Account Safety Protocol

### Protecting the Account
- **2FA enabled** — mandatory, no exceptions
- **Email + phone** both verified on account
- **No third-party bots** — TikTok bans for fake engagement; only organic or paid-TikTok tools
- **Community Guidelines review** — read TikTok's guidelines quarterly; the algorithm suppresses accounts with violations even without a ban
- **Backup content** — always have 5–7 videos pre-recorded as buffer; never scramble to post

### If the Account Gets Flagged
1. Do NOT delete flagged video immediately — appeal first via TikTok's in-app tool
2. Review the specific guideline cited
3. Pause posting for 24 hours after a warning
4. If shadowbanned (views drop suddenly): stop posting for 48–72 hours, then resume with highly safe content
