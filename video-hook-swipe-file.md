# Video Hook Swipe File — @ilo.krafts
**Use this as your go-to reference before filming any video.**
Every hook here is tested on crafts/maker/small-biz content.

---

## THE RULE: First 2 Seconds = Everything
TikTok decides whether to push your video in the first loop.
If the hook doesn't stop the scroll, nothing else matters.

---

## CATEGORY 1 — Curiosity Hooks (make them need to know what happens)
1. "I wasn't going to post this, but..."
2. "Nobody talks about how long this actually takes."
3. "Watch until the end. The reveal is everything."
4. "I was NOT expecting this to work."
5. "She almost didn't share this with us."
6. "The part they never show you on craft videos."
7. "This is why handmade costs what it costs."
8. "Before you buy [product] anywhere — watch this."
9. "You've been buying the wrong version of this."
10. "I had to sit down after seeing this for the first time."

---

## CATEGORY 2 — Identity/POV Hooks (speak directly to who they are)
1. "POV: You finally find a gift that actually means something."
2. "POV: You discover handmade shopping and can't go back."
3. "If you've ever felt guilty buying fast fashion, this is for you."
4. "This video is for people who give the best gifts."
5. "If you can spot the difference between handmade and machine-made, you're our people."
6. "For everyone who feels something when they hold something handmade."
7. "POV: You're the person in the group who finds the most unique things."
8. "This one's for the 'I don't want something from Amazon' people."

---

## CATEGORY 3 — Contrast/Comparison Hooks (create tension)
1. "Same product. $8 vs $45. Here's the real difference."
2. "This took 8 hours to make. It'll outlast a $200 [brand name] version."
3. "Fast fashion vs handmade. Let me show you what you're actually buying."
4. "Factory-made vs hand-made — side by side. You tell me."
5. "She makes this in 3 days. A factory makes 3,000 a day. Guess which one lasts."

---

## CATEGORY 4 — Specificity Hooks (specific = credible)
1. "She shipped 47 orders last week from a studio the size of a bathroom."
2. "This single piece took 14 hours. It sold in 6 minutes."
3. "Every stitch in this is hand-placed. There are 847 of them."
4. "She's been doing this for 11 years and still gets nervous before she sells."
5. "He quit his corporate job to make these. Best decision of his life."
6. "This sold out 3 times in the same week."
7. "She has a 6-week waitlist and still prices this at $40."

---

## CATEGORY 5 — Problem/Solution Hooks (speak to a pain point)
1. "You've been scrolling for a gift for 3 hours. Let me end that."
2. "Everything you find on Amazon looks the same. Here's the alternative."
3. "You want something no one else has. I found it."
4. "Gift giving is hard. Handmade makes it easy."
5. "You don't need to spend $200 to give a thoughtful gift."
6. "Tired of giving gifts that get used once and forgotten? Watch this."

---

## CATEGORY 6 — Emotional/Story Hooks (highest share potential)
1. "She started making these when she had nothing. Now she can't keep up with orders."
2. "He learned this craft from his grandmother. She passed last year. He still makes it."
3. "A customer bought this as her mom's last birthday gift. She messaged us after."
4. "She almost shut her shop down. Then one video changed everything."
5. "He doesn't have a website. Just ilo.krafts. And a 3-month waitlist."

---

## CATEGORY 7 — Education/Value Hooks (drives saves)
1. "Three things to look for before buying handmade anywhere."
2. "How to tell if something is actually handmade or just labeled that way."
3. "The craft technique most people get wrong — and how to fix it."
4. "What 'handmade to order' actually means (and why it's better)."
5. "How to find the best handmade vendors before everyone else does."

---

## TEXT OVERLAY RULES
- Font: Bold. Big. Readable on a moving background.
- Max 6 words on screen at once.
- Contrast: White text + black stroke, OR dark text on light background.
- Position: Top 1/3 of screen (safe zone — not covered by UI buttons).

## AUDIO RULES
- Always check trending sounds on Friday for the following week's culture videos.
- Save trending sounds to favorites immediately — they disappear.
- For process/craft videos: use soft, satisfying ambient sound or trending lo-fi.
- For emotional stories: slower, melodic trending audio.
- For product reveals: something with a satisfying beat drop for the reveal moment.
